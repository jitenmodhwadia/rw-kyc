Re-KYC and consent form · Effective 11 November 2026

This notice explains how Richwealth (“we”), Ranmal Lake, Gate No. 1, Jamnagar 361001, Gujarat, handles the personal information you share through the re-KYC and consent form.

1. Information we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and email address (optional).
  • Photos of your PAN card and Aadhaar card (front and back), and your photo / selfie.
  • Your current address (if different from Aadhaar), and whether your bank account, nominee or Privé membership details need updating.
  • Your consent record: the wording you accepted, language, consent version, date and time, typed name and place.
  • Basic technical details such as your device and browser type.

2. Why we use it

  • To verify your identity and keep your client records up to date.
  • To process payouts to your verified bank account.
  • To deduct and deposit TDS and meet other legal and tax requirements.
  • To contact you about your account, verification, payouts, tax documents and service updates.
  • To set up your Richwealth app account when the app is available.
  • To send information about offers, memberships, projects and events — only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.

3. How we store and protect it

Your documents and details are stored in Richwealth’s private, access-controlled storage. Only authorised Richwealth staff who need them for the purposes above can view them. We do not use your Aadhaar for any purpose other than identity verification.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed: with banks to make your payouts, with tax authorities and other government bodies where the law requires, with professional advisers (such as auditors), and with service providers who store or process data on our behalf under confidentiality.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are associated with Richwealth and afterwards for as long as required by tax and other laws, or to resolve any dispute.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask to see or correct your information, update your bank or nominee details, withdraw your consent to promotional messages, or raise a concern about how your information is handled, as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Withdrawing consent needed for KYC, payouts or tax compliance may affect our ability to make payouts.
